Procedure

  1. I
    Policy

    The community writes numbered rules, sets an appeal bond and an overturn reward, and funds the pool.

  2. II
    Action

    A moderator records member, rule, action and the content's SHA-256. One reward is reserved from the pool.

  3. III
    Appeal

    Within 7 days the member pays the bond and submits the exact content (hash must match) and their side.

  4. IV
    Validators judge

    Each lists the rules the content violates; they must agree whether that list is empty.

Overturnedno rule violated: bond back plus the reward
Uphelda rule violated: the bond goes to the pool
