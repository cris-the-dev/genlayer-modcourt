Procedure

  1. I
    Policy

    The community writes numbered rules, each with the strongest action it allows, sets an appeal bond and an overturn reward, and funds the pool.

  2. II
    Signed post

    The member's wallet signs a commitment to the post's SHA-256. A moderator can only act on a hash its author signed.

  3. III
    Appeal

    Within 7 days the member pays the exact bond. That registers the appeal; nothing in it can fail on the network.

  4. IV
    Ruling

    Within 3 days the member points to a copy of the post. Validators fetch it, check the hash and agree on one question: does it violate the rule the moderator cited? A failed attempt changes nothing and can be retried.

  5. V
    Deadline

    No ruling by the deadline: anyone can refund the bond; the action stands, unreviewed.

Overturnedcited rule not violated: bond back plus the reward
Upheldcited rule violated: the bond goes to the bond fund

Disclosures

Where bonds goAn upheld bond goes to the community's bond fund. It pays future overturn rewards first and the owner can never withdraw it; the owner can withdraw only pool money not reserved for open cases.
SafeguardsEach action reserves one reward, so a community cannot act beyond what it can pay for mistakes. A rule's cap on the action is enforced when the action is recorded. A moderator with 3 or more overturned actions, more than half of their appealed ones, is suspended and the owner cannot reinstate them.
What the verdict meansValidators agree only on whether the cited rule was violated. The one-sentence explanation is the leader's own and is not checked by consensus.
PrivacyThe contract stores neither the post nor your statement. Your statement is still part of the transaction, public and permanent. The post is fetched from your URL during the ruling; take the page down afterwards if you like.
Sign a post (members)

Your wallet signs that this exact text is your post in this community. Give the hash and signature to the moderators; ModCourt only accepts actions on posts their author signed.
